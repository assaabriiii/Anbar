# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, the contributors and maintainers of Anbar, pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps build a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences, including different languages. Both English and Persian are welcome in issues and discussions.
- Giving constructive feedback, and accepting it gracefully
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Asking people to take personal or legal risks, for example to bypass restrictions in ways that could endanger them
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately through GitHub (a [private security advisory](https://github.com/assaabriiii/Anbar/security/advisories/new) also works for sensitive cases). All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. They may also warn, or temporarily or permanently ban, any contributor whose behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
